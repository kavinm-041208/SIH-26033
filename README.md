## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
AgriConnect is an online agricultural trading platform developed to make the selling and purchasing of farm products easier. It provides a common space where farmers can display their products and buyers can purchase them directly.

In the traditional marketing system, agricultural products pass through several trading stages before reaching consumers. This increases product prices and reduces the amount received by farmers. AgriConnect aims to simplify this process by providing direct communication between producers and buyers.

The platform allows farmers to upload crop information, including product images, available stock, location, and expected selling price. Buyers can view the listed products and select the products according to their requirements.

The system also provides useful market information to help farmers make better selling decisions.

Important Features:

    Farmer Product Listing: Farmers can publish their available crops with price, quantity, and quality details.

    Buyer Dashboard: Customers and wholesale buyers can explore agricultural products in one place.

    Market Price Information: Displays available price information to help farmers understand market conditions.

    Order Management: Maintains product orders and helps farmers manage their sales.

    AI Demand Estimation: Analyzes previous sales information to estimate the demand for agricultural products.

Speciality of the Project:

The main speciality of AgriConnect is that it focuses on simplifying agricultural trading rather than depending on traditional selling methods. It combines product management, buyer communication, and intelligent market analysis in a single platform.
## Technical Approach
AgriConnect will be developed using web technologies to provide an easy-to-access platform for farmers and buyers.

Software Requirements:

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript

Backend: Python Django

Database: MySQL

Machine Learning: Python and Scikit-learn

Data Processing: Pandas and NumPy

Development Environment: Visual Studio Code

Version Control: GitHub

Development Methodology:

1. User Interface Development

A simple and responsive website will be designed for farmers and customers. The interface will contain separate sections for registration, login, product details, and order management.

2. Database Creation

A database will be created to store user information, agricultural product details, prices, quantities, and purchase records.

3. Product Management

Farmers can add new products, edit existing information, and update the available quantity whenever necessary.

4. Buyer Interaction

Buyers can search for products, check their details, and send purchase requests through the platform.

5. AI Integration

A machine learning model can analyze historical sales records and identify demand patterns. Based on the available data, it can provide estimated demand information for different crops.

6. Testing and Deployment

The application will be tested to check user registration, product uploads, database operations, and order processing before deployment.

## Feasibility and Viability
AgriConnect can be developed using commonly available technologies without requiring expensive hardware or specialized infrastructure.

Implementation Feasibility:

Python Django provides the necessary tools for building the application, while MySQL can manage the required agricultural and customer information. Machine learning libraries can be integrated for basic demand analysis.

Challenges and Solutions:

    Low Digital Awareness: Some farmers may find online platforms difficult to use. A simple interface and regional language options can make the application easier to understand.

    Changing Product Prices: Agricultural prices may vary depending on season and availability. Providing updated market information can help users make informed decisions.

    Data Availability: AI predictions may be limited when sufficient sales records are unavailable. The system can initially use sample datasets and improve as actual transaction data increases.

    Product Quality: Buyers may be concerned about the quality of products. Farmers can upload product images and relevant quality details before listing them.

    Application Accessibility: Rural users may experience slow internet connectivity. A lightweight and mobile-friendly website can improve accessibility.

Future Scope:

In the future, the platform can be extended with mobile application support, digital payment integration, transport service connections, and advanced crop demand prediction.

## Impact and Benefits
AgriConnect can improve the way agricultural products are marketed and distributed by making information available directly to farmers and buyers.

Farmer Development:

    Helps small farmers reach more potential buyers.

    Provides an additional online selling opportunity.

    Reduces dependence on traditional marketing channels.

    Helps farmers understand product demand and market prices.

    Supports better management of agricultural sales.

Consumer Advantages:

    Makes agricultural products easier to find.

    Provides product and price information before purchasing.

    Creates opportunities to purchase directly from producers.

    Helps reduce additional costs in the supply chain.

Agricultural Development:

    Encourages the use of digital platforms in farming.

    Supports technology adoption among rural communities.

    Improves communication between producers and buyers.

    Promotes organized agricultural product marketing.

Long-Term Benefits:

The platform can help develop a more connected agricultural trading network. It can also support FPOs, local retailers, and wholesale purchasers by providing a common digital marketplace
